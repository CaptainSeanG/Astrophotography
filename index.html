About the gallery

Sean Gallagher Astrophotography brings together finished photographs from my deep-sky imaging library. The collection includes nearby stellar remnants and galaxies millions of light-years away.

Acquisition and processing

I use N.I.N.A. for acquisition and PixInsight and Astro Automation for processing. Integration times come from the processing records for the displayed photograph.
